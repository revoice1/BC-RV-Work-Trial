# 💻 Your Windows PC Is Moving to Our New Device Management

> 📅 **Between [START DATE] and [END DATE]** you'll see a prompt from **AiLense IT** asking you to sign in. It moves your PC to our new device management system.

> ⚠️ **Before anything else: do you know your AiLense SSO password?**
> If you normally sign in to Windows with a **PIN, fingerprint, or face (Windows Hello)**, you may not have typed your password in a while. **You'll need it for this.**
> Not sure? Sign in to [Google](https://mail.google.com) in a private browser window to test it now. If it doesn't work, contact us in **#it-support** *before* the prompt appears.

## What's happening?

AiLense is moving all company computers to a new device management system. It keeps your PC secure and up to date, and it's part of getting AiLense ready for our SOC 2 audit.

**Your files, apps, and settings stay exactly where they are.** Nothing is erased.

**Good news for Windows users:** this is quick. There are no setup screens, and you can keep working while it finishes.

## ✅ Before You Start

- [ ] **Set aside about 15 minutes.** It usually takes less, and your PC may need to restart at the end.
- [ ] **Know your AiLense SSO password** (see above).
- [ ] **Have your phone or YubiKey with you** for the security check (MFA).
- [ ] **Have a good internet connection.** Home or office Wi-Fi, not a flight or spotty hotspot.
- [ ] **Plug in your charger.**

## 🚀 Step by Step

### 1. Watch for the prompt

A window from **AiLense IT** appears asking you to sign in to the new device management system.

> 💡 Not a good time? You can postpone it. The prompt will come back. Please complete it before **[END DATE]**.

### 2. Sign in with your AiLense account

1. Enter your **AiLense email address**
2. Enter your **AiLense SSO password**
3. Complete the security check:
   - **Phone:** approve the sign-in request on your phone
   - **YubiKey:** insert it and tap it when prompted

### 3. Let it finish

Setup continues in the background. **You can keep working.** You may see a few apps or settings update over the next hour.

### 4. Restart if asked

If you're prompted to restart, **save your work first**, then restart. After the restart, sign in as usual. You're done.

## 🆘 Need Help?

> 🚨 **If you see an error, the prompt doesn't appear by [END DATE], or anything looks wrong, message us in [#it-support](#) on Slack ASAP.**
> Include a screenshot or a photo of the screen if you can.

**Watch out for phishing:** the prompt comes from **AiLense IT** on your PC. IT will **never** ask for your password over Slack, email, or phone.
