# 💻 Your Mac Is Moving to Our New Device Management

> 📅 **Complete by [DATE].**
> You'll see a macOS prompt asking you to move your Mac to our new device management system. Please do it on your own time before the deadline.

> ⚠️ **Before anything else: do you know your AiLense SSO password?**
> If you normally unlock your Mac with Touch ID or sign in to apps with saved passwords, you may not have typed it in a while. **You'll need it during this process**, and you won't be able to look it up on your Mac while the migration runs.
> Not sure? Sign in to [Google](https://mail.google.com) in a private browser window to test it now. If it doesn't work, contact us in **#it-support** *before* you start.

## What's happening?

AiLense is moving all company Macs to a new device management system. It keeps your Mac secure and up to date, and it's part of getting AiLense ready for our SOC 2 audit.

**Your files, apps, and settings stay exactly where they are.** Nothing is erased.

## ✅ Before You Start

- [ ] **Set aside 20–30 minutes.** It usually takes only a few minutes, but give yourself room in case you hit a snag. **Please don't start right before a meeting.**
- [ ] **Know your AiLense SSO password** (see above).
- [ ] **Have your phone or YubiKey with you** for the security check (MFA).
- [ ] **Have a good internet connection.** Home or office Wi-Fi is ideal. **Not on a flight**, hotspot, or spotty café Wi-Fi.
- [ ] **Plug in your charger.**
- [ ] **Save your work and close your apps.**

## 🚀 Step by Step

### 1. Start the migration

You'll see a **macOS notification about device management**. Click it, or open **System Settings** and follow the prompt.

> 💡 Only start when you've checked everything in **Before You Start**.

### 2. Your Mac switches to setup screens

Your Mac will show screens that **look like the setup you saw when you first got your Mac**. This is expected.

> 🔒 **You won't have normal access to your Mac during this process.** You can't open apps, files, or your password manager until it finishes.

### 3. Sign in with your AiLense account

When asked:

1. Enter your **AiLense email address**
2. Enter your **AiLense SSO password**
3. Complete the security check:
   - **Phone:** approve the sign-in request on your phone
   - **YubiKey:** insert it and tap it when prompted

### 4. Let it finish

Your Mac enrolls in the new system. **Don't close the lid or shut down.** This usually takes a few minutes.

### 5. You're back

When the migration is complete, **you're returned to your usual login screen and profile**, with everything where you left it.

Over the next hour you may see a few apps update or install in the background. That's normal.

## 🆘 Need Help?

> 🚨 **If you see an error, get stuck on a screen, or anything looks wrong, message us in [#it-support](#) on Slack ASAP.**
> Include a photo of the screen if you can (your phone works great for this).
> If Slack isn't working on your Mac, use Slack on your phone.

**Watch out for phishing:** IT will **never** ask for your password over Slack, email, or phone.
